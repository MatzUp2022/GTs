# 3V3 Arena — Event Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- Cross-server event (e.g. #147/#149/#151/#154), open for **14 days** per opening.
- Eligibility: the server's **top 200 players by Total Hero CP** — automatic; you can join mid-event as soon as your CP qualifies.
- Everyone starts with **1,000 points**; initial ranking by Total Hero CP.

## 2. Points & Battles

- Each challenge offers multiple opponents. Win = points (**more against higher-point opponents**); loss = deduction (**more against lower-point opponents**).
- **Best-of-three:** your 3 attack teams against the opponent's 3 defense teams.
- Final ranking is determined by points.

## 3. Defense

- Default defense = teams 1, 2 and 3; adjustable in the Defense menu after the start.
- The arena defense is **saved separately** and does not affect your other teams.

## 4. Rewards

- **Daily:** victory chests after 1 / 3 / 5 wins (incl. 50–100 diamonds, medals, chests, stamina).
- **Season ranking (excerpt):**

| Rank | Rewards (among others) |
|------|------------------------|
| 1 | Turret skin · 3 gifts · 3,000 stamina · 30K medals |
| 2 | Turret ×1 · 2 · 2,000 · 25K |
| 3 | Turret ×1 · 2 · 1,500 · 20K |
| 4–10 | Turret ×1 · 1 · 1,000 · 15K |
| 11–20 | Turret ×1 · 1 · 950 · 14K |
| 21–40 | 3 gifts · 900 stamina · 13K · 3,000 diamonds |
| 41–70 | 2 · 850 · 12K · 3,000 diamonds |

- **Alliance rewards:** alliances with top-10 commanders receive extra rewards at the end (each tier only once; rank 1 incl. 1,000 stamina + a 150 chest).

## 5. Checklist

- [ ] 5 wins daily for all victory chests
- [ ] 3 attack teams set up thoughtfully for the best-of-three
- [ ] Arena defense maintained (saved separately)
- [ ] Prefer attacking opponents with more points
- [ ] Push CP if not yet in the top 200
