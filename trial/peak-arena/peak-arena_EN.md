# Peak Arena — Event Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- Opens **every Tuesday**; matches run for 3 days and close after Friday; **offseason Sat–Mon**.
- Eligibility: the server's **top 200 players by Total Hero CP** — automatic; joining midway is possible as soon as your CP qualifies.
- Each round includes commanders from **1–4 regions**.
- Initial ranking based on your CP when entering.

## 2. Battle & Ranking

- Each challenge offers multiple opponents. Defeating a higher-ranked opponent → **the rankings are swapped**.
- **Limited challenges per day** — aim as high as possible instead of collecting safe small wins.
- Season rewards are issued on Friday after the matches end.

## 3. Defense

- Default defense = your strongest sandbox team; adjustable in the Defense menu after the start.
- The Peak Arena defense is **saved separately** and does not affect your other teams.

## 4. Rewards

- **Daily:** victory chests after 1 / 3 / 5 wins (Challenge screen).
- **Season:** rewards by final ranking, issued on Friday.

## 5. Checklist

- [ ] Tuesday: check entry (top 200 by hero CP?)
- [ ] Daily challenges fully used (limited!)
- [ ] Attack higher-ranked opponents for the rank swap
- [ ] Defense team maintained separately
- [ ] 1/3/5 wins daily for the victory chests
