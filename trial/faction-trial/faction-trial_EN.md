# Faction Trial — Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- Three trials: **Guardian, Ranger, Berserker** — in each trial the matching faction deals **+50 % DMG**.
- Progress resets **every week on Tue / Thu / Sun**.
- Difficulty is selectable, but: **only one level per cycle, and it cannot be changed afterwards.**
- **Each hero can only participate in one trial** — the roster has to be split across the three trials.
- **Free bonus:** since every trial team is mono-faction anyway, the line-up bonus of **+20 % HP/ATK/DEF** applies automatically.

## 2. Recommended Sequence

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Split the roster into three faction teams in advance (no hero twice) |
| 2 | Pick each trial's difficulty deliberately — check the enemy CP (e.g. Lv. 16 ≈ 9.2M) and go as high as safely possible |
| 3 | Finish all three trials before the respective reset |
| 4 | Collect achievements and check the store offers |

- Tip: the three faction teams match the boss weaknesses in the Frenzied Boss event (Guardian/Ranger/Berserker) — reuse the setups.

## 3. Rewards

- "Total Rewards for Clearing" grow with the level (e.g. 25 EXP/iron/bread chests + 5 special chests per clear).
- Each trial has a **leaderboard**, plus **achievement** chests and a **store**.

## 4. Checklist

- [ ] Three faction teams planned (no hero in two trials)
- [ ] Difficulty picked per trial (cannot be changed within the cycle!)
- [ ] Guardian, Ranger and Berserker trials played before the reset
- [ ] Achievements and store checked
