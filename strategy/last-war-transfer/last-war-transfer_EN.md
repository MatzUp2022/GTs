# Transferred Strategy — from Last War: Survival (Last Light)

*As of September 2026 there are **no public guides for Last Light itself** — searches only turn up the store listing and the Facebook page. The rules below come from guides to **Last War: Survival**, whose event systems are nearly identical. **None of it is verified in Last Light** — only move a figure into an event manual once it is confirmed in game (rule 7).*

## 1. Why the Comparison Holds

| System | Last War: Survival | Last Light (documented here) |
|--------|--------------------|------------------------------|
| Arms Race | 6 phases of 4 h, reset at 0:00 server time | **identical** |
| Phase themes | Hero Advancement · City Building · Unit Progression · Tech Research · **Drone Boost** | Boost Heroes · Construct City · Expand Army · Research Tech · **Boost Vehicles** |
| Alliance duel | 6 themed days Mon–Sat | **Our Alliance Duel — same shape, now documented** (Mon–Sat, 13 win points) |
| Stamina | save it for the drone phase | radar auto-complete, 10 per mission |

→ Apart from the names the systems map one to one — the drone corresponds functionally to our **vehicle**.

## 2. Core Rule: Timing Beats Spending

- Players who place actions into the matching phase score **2–3×** what the same spending scores unplanned. "The event rewards timing, not spending."
- **The most valuable technique:** start a building or research **before** the phase and use speedups so the **completion lands inside it**.
- Phase chests are **not** claimed automatically — collect them manually after each phase.
- The single biggest leak according to the guides: **burning stamina outside the matching phase**.

## 3. Speedup Stockpiles (targets)

| Stock | F2P minimum | Competitive |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| Generic | 50 h | 100 h |
| Construction | 30 h | 60 h |
| Research | 30 h | 60 h |
| Training | 50 h | 100 h |
| Healing | 20 h | 40 h |

- Before a season or SvS start the guides recommend **200+ h in total**.
- The only exception to hoarding: a base milestone that unlocks new content.

## 4. Directly Applicable to Our Events

| Transferred rule | Apply in Last Light to |
|------------------|------------------------|
| Spend nothing outside the matching phase | Arms Race (6 phases), speedups, research completions |
| Stamina only in the matching phase | radar auto-complete (10 per mission) |
| Double scoring when two events reward the same action | Arms Race "Research Tech" **+** Alliance Duel research incentive **+** Region Duel points — the same research counts three times |
| Scout before attacking | Region Duel, Central City Clash |
| Open event items only on the right day | chests, tickets, resource packs |

## 5. What Does **Not** Transfer

- Concrete point values, hero names, tier lists and reward tables — Last Light has its own roster (26 heroes) and its own numbers, held in the hero database and the event manuals.
- **Superseded:** this used to be compared against the Region Duel. Last War's duel week (Mon–Sat, 12 victory points) is in fact the counterpart of **our own Alliance Duel** (Mon–Sat, 13 win points), which is now documented from in-game data — use that manual, not this transfer.
