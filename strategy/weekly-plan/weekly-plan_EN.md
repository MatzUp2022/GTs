# Weekly Plan — Which Day, Which Hour (Last Light)

*All times are **server time**. Sources: the manuals of the individual events; where this plan draws its own conclusion it is marked.*

## 1. Two Logins Carry the Week

- **8:00 and 20:00.** Twelve hours apart — below the Adventure storage cap of **14.57 h**, so nothing overflows.
- Both land on an Arms Race phase boundary, and from Tue to Fri on the phase that matches the day's Alliance Duel theme (section 3).
- Only one login possible? Take the **evening one before 24:00** — at 24:00 undefeated Frenzied Bosses flee and the Alliance Duel day settles.

## 2. The Fixed Clock

| Time | What happens | What to do |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **before 0:00** | Top Alliance daily rewards expire | **Claim — unclaimed rewards reset the missions too** |
| **0:00** | Day rollover: Arms Race phase 1 · Alliance Duel theme change · tavern and Top Alliance missions refresh | Fill tavern slots (Golden refresh first) |
| **0 / 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20** | Arms Race phase change, 4 h each | Big spends **only** inside the matching phase |
| **every 6 h** | Radar refresh | Storage caps around **35** — work it off |
| **0 / 6 / 12 / 18** | World Boss (**Mon · Thu · Sun**), 3 h each | 5 hits, only the best one counts |
| **0 / 6 / 12** | Frenzied Boss spawns | **At 24:00 all undefeated bosses flee** |
| **~23:30** | Alliance Boss auto-starts if R4/R5 set no time | R4/R5: set the time when the alliance is online |

## 3. The Double-Scoring Window

Arms Race runs a fixed 7-day rotation, the Alliance Duel runs Mon–Sat. **If Arms Race day 1 is a Monday**, the phases at **8:00 and 20:00** (both columns are identical) match the Alliance Duel theme on four days in a row:

| Day | Alliance Duel theme | Arms Race 8:00 & 20:00 | One spend scores twice |
|-----|---------------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| Tue | Base Construction | Construct City | building upgrades + speedups |
| Wed | Tech Research | Research Tech | research + speedups |
| Thu | Hero Upgrade | Boost Heroes | hero EXP, skills, stars |
| Fri | Full Mobilization | Expand Army | troop training + speedups |

Mon (Radar) and Sat (Defeat Enemies) have **no** Arms Race counterpart — and are exactly the two days that do not match. That the four matchable days all line up is what makes the assumption plausible.

**Verify once:** on a Tuesday, check the 8:00 phase. *Construct City* → the table holds every week. Something else → count the offset forward once; the rotation is fixed. **[assumption — the game does not state the link between rotation day and weekday]**

→ Consequence: **hero EXP belongs to Thursday, not Monday.** Monday's 8:00 phase is Boost Heroes as well, but only Thursday additionally pays Alliance Duel points for it.

## 4. The Week

| Day | Alliance Duel (win pts) | Also running | The one thing |
|-----|------------------------|--------------|---------------|
| **Mon** | Radar Training (1) | **World Boss** · Arms Race | Radar missions — **25,000 duel points each**, everything else is a rounding error |
| **Tue** | Base Construction (2) | **Peak Arena opens** · Faction Trial reset | **Open the buildings left in chest state since Friday** |
| **Wed** | Tech Research (2) | Peak Arena · Vehicle chests | Fire the saved research speedups in the 8:00 / 20:00 window |
| **Thu** | Hero Upgrade (2) | **World Boss** · Faction Trial reset | All hero recruit tickets, levels, skills, stars — **hold radar missions back for Friday** |
| **Fri** | Full Mobilization (2) | Peak Arena closes · *(SvS settlement)* | The saved low-level soldiers + the held-back radar missions. **From here on leave finished buildings in chest state** |
| **Sat** | **Defeat Enemies (4)** | Alliance Raid · *(SvS Central City Clash)* | The day that decides the duel. Not fighting → **shield up** |
| **Sun** | — matchmaking | **World Boss** · Faction Trial reset | Alliance CP on Sunday decides whether you make the **top 32** for next week's duel |

**13 win points across the week; Saturday alone is worth 4.** Lose Saturday and you have to win four of the remaining five days.

## 5. Save Instead of Spend

Almost every manual says the same thing in its own words: value lies in the timing, not the amount.

| Save | Until |
|------|-------|
| Low-level soldiers | **Friday** (Full Mobilization) |
| Finished buildings, left in chest state | **Tuesday** (Base Construction) |
| Radar missions | Thursday → **Friday** |
| Construction / research / training speedups | the matching **Arms Race phase**, ideally 8:00 or 20:00 |
| Hero recruit tickets · Survivor recruit tickets | **Thursday** · **Tuesday** |
| Vehicle chests | **Wednesday** |

## 6. SvS Weeks (optional — the Region Duel does not run every week)

The Region Duel overlays the week without replacing it:

- **Mon–Fri points contest.** World Boss is the whole game: up to **+400,000 region points per day**, against +30,000 for an Alliance Duel win and +2,000 for Arms Race rank 1. On **Mon / Thu / Sun** everything else steps back.
- **Friday settlement** decides invader vs. defender.
- **From Saturday Central City Clash** — same day as Alliance Duel "Defeat Enemies" and Alliance Raid. Every member needs **50,000 personal points** for the win-streak reward.

→ In an SvS week the plan does not change. Only World Boss moves above everything else on Mon, Thu and Sun.
