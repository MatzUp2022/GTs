# Region Duel (SvS) — Event Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- **1v1 duel between two regions (servers).** Victory = **occupying the opposing region's Central City**.
- Several regions are matched into one group and play a **2–3 week** tournament until a champion region emerges (winners face winners, losers face losers).
- Off-season: random matchmaking; in-season: matchmaking by season ranking.
- **Unity decides:** commanders from the same region do not attack each other at neutral buildings — they are allies (except for direct base attacks).

## 2. Weekly Rhythm

| Period | Phase | What happens |
|--------|-------|--------------|
| Mon–Fri | **Points contest** | Win events and collect region points |
| Fri | Settlement | Region with more points = **invader**, fewer = **defender** |
| From Sat | **Central City Clash** | Invaders can relocate to the enemy region and seize its Central City |

## 3. Region Points (Mon–Fri)

| Source | Points |
|--------|--------|
| **World Boss:** region with highest total damage | **+250,000** |
| **World Boss:** rank 1 in personal damage | **+150,000** |
| Alliance Duel victory | +30,000 |
| MVP in Alliance Duel | +6,000 |
| Rank 1 in the Arms Race | +2,000 |
| Plunder an enemy region's truck | +100 |

→ **World Boss dominates the scoring** (up to +400,000/day) — see the World Boss manual: it has top priority during SvS.

## 4. Central City Clash (from Saturday)

> **Not to be confused with [Central City Battle](https://github.com/MatzUp2022/GTs/blob/main/events/central-city-battle/central-city-battle_EN.md)** — the weekly 8-hour contest in which alliances holding a Lv. 6 city fight for the Central City and the winner's leader becomes Governor. Whether the Saturday clash below is that same event during an SvS week is **[assumption]** — see that manual.

**Personal point sources (excerpt):**

| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Knock up an enemy commander base | +50,000 |
| Kill a Lv. 8 soldier in the Central City / Giant Battery | +54 |
| Kill a Lv. 8 soldier in the Polluted Zone | +27 |
| Kill a Lv. 8 soldier in general | +9 |
| Reduce an enemy base's defense points by 1 | +10 |
| Garrison in Giant Battery / Central City (per second, on return) | +25 |

- The region's **win streak reward** requires **50,000 personal points** — minimum participation for everyone.
- Personal point chests from **125,000 / 250,000 / 375,000** points.
- Defenders: hold and garrison the Central City; invaders: relocate together and take the Central City.

## 6. Checklist

- [ ] Mon–Fri: World Boss played in full every day — **during SvS it runs daily** (biggest point source)
- [ ] Alliance Duel & Arms Race played to win
- [ ] Enemy trucks plundered
- [ ] Sat: 50,000 personal points collected for the win streak reward
- [ ] Kills focused on Central City / Giant Battery (+54 instead of +9)
