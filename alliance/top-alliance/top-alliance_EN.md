# Top Alliance & Alliance Promotion — Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Rating Ladder

- Alliance ratings: **Temporary → Basic → Intermediate → Premium → Top.**
- You climb via **promotion missions and stars**.

## 2. Promotion Rules

- Incomplete promotion missions refresh at **0:00 server time**.
- **All missions done = 1 star**; at **3 stars** the rating can be increased.
- After gaining a star, new missions only unlock **the next day**.
- Once completed, **all members** can claim the rewards — after the promotion they **expire**; each character may claim only **1 promotion reward**.

## 3. Top Alliance Daily Missions (examples)

| Mission | Reward (among others) |
|---------|-----------------------|
| 200 alliance tech donations (total) | 5,000 gold · 100 medals |
| 30 messages in the alliance channel | 3,000 iron · 3,000 bread · 100 medals |
| 30 rally monsters defeated | 5m speedups · medals |

- **All daily missions done** → daily mission rewards (incl. 500 medals, a task item, 5 chests, 5 boxes).
- **Warning:** if the daily rewards are **not claimed, both rewards and missions reset** — claiming is mandatory!

## 4. Checklist (daily, every member)

- [ ] Alliance tech donation made (counts toward the 200)
- [ ] Posted in the alliance channel (counts toward the 30)
- [ ] Joined rally monster kills (counts toward the 30)
- [ ] Daily mission reward claimed (otherwise reset!)
