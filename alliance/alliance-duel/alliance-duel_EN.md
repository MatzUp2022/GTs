# Alliance Duel (VS) — Event Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- **Alliance vs. alliance over six days** (Day 1–6). One theme per day; only actions matching that day's theme score. Opened via the **VS button** on the map.
- **Requirements:** Base Level **10+** to take part · the alliance needs **at least 20 members** · only the **top 32 alliances by CP per region** enter Sunday's matchmaking for the coming week.
- **Winner takes all, per day:** the alliance with more points that day **keeps** its points — the loser **loses** theirs.
- **Personal points are never affected.** Personal chests stay claimable after a lost day, and an alliance change during the duel only blocks **alliance** point gains for 1 day.

## 2. Win Points per Day — Saturday decides

| Day | Theme | Win points |
|-----|-------|------------|
| 1 | Radar Training | 1 |
| 2 | Base Construction | 2 |
| 3 | Tech Research | 2 |
| 4 | Hero Upgrade | 2 |
| 5 | Full Mobilization | 2 |
| 6 | **Defeat Enemies** | **4** |

- **13 win points in total.** Day 6 alone is worth as much as Days 1–3 together.
- → Lose Day 6 and you have to win **four of the remaining five days**. Plan the week backwards from Saturday. **[assumption — the table is from Duel Results; the majority arithmetic is our own]**

## 3. Point Economy — measured on Day 1 (Radar Training)

| Task | Points |
|------|--------|
| **Complete 1 Radar Mission** | **+25,000** |
| Consume 1 Vehicle Upgrade Part | +5,000 |
| Consume 1 Stamina | +300 |
| Gather 100 Food / 100 Iron / 60 Gold (radar missions excluded) | +40 each |
| Purchase a pack containing Diamonds (1 diamond) | +30 |
| Consume 1 Vehicle EXP | +6 |
| Consume 660 Hero EXP | +2 |

- **One radar mission = 625× a 100-food gather.** Next to radar missions and vehicle parts, everything else is rounding error.
- Personal chests at **40,000 / 150,000 / 540,000** points → rewards 8,000 · 10,000 · 18,000 💎. That is **2 · 6 · 22 radar missions**.
- Radar missions cost stamina, and stamina scores on its own (+300) — the same stamina pays twice.

## 4. Daily Plan

| Day | Theme | What scores | Hold back for later |
|-----|-------|-------------|---------------------|
| 1 | Radar Training | Radar missions · stamina · vehicle levels · hero levels | low-level soldiers → Day 5 |
| 2 | Base Construction | Building upgrades with speedups · Tavern: refresh to **Golden** before dispatch · dispatch **S+ trucks only** · all Survivor Recruit Tickets | low-level soldiers → Day 5 |
| 3 | Tech Research | Research with speedups · radar missions · open Vehicle Chests | low-level soldiers → Day 5 |
| 4 | Hero Upgrade | All Hero Recruit Tickets · hero levels, skills and stars | **radar missions** + low-level soldiers → Day 5 |
| 5 | Full Mobilization | Train soldiers and upgrade low-level ones with speedups · the saved radar missions | finished buildings **left in chest state** → Day 2 next week |
| 6 | Defeat Enemies | Attacks · assisting allies in defense · S+ trucks · training, building and healing with speedups | — |

Two standing rules from the game's own tips:
- **Save low-level soldiers all week for Day 5** — upgrading them is what Full Mobilization pays for.
- **Do not claim finished buildings.** Leave them in chest state and open them on Day 2.

**Day 6 specifics:** not fighting → **shield up**. Huge power gap against the opponent → hit other small alliances instead. **Alliance Raid** opens together with the Day 6 theme: move into the opposing alliance's region to fight there while covering your own.

## 5. Chests & Tech

- Every **Duel Chest contains Energy Cores** — the key material for premium tech. Open as many as possible every day.
- **Alliance Duel Tech** in the Research Lab unlocks more duel chests and raises the point yield per action (+5 → +50 % over 10 levels). Research Incentive first — see the [Research: Alliance Duel](https://github.com/MatzUp2022/GTs/blob/main/base/research/alliance-duel/alliance-duel_EN.md) manual.

## 6. Duel League

- Servers **open for more than 7 weeks** join at season start; only the **top 16 alliances in the region** participate.
- Ranking: more wins → whoever won earlier → random.
- Promotion and demotion after each duel, based on the group ranking.
- Rewards arrive **~1 hour after settlement**. **Leaving the alliance before settlement forfeits them**, and there is a **minimum point total** you must reach.

## 7. Link to SvS

- **Alliance Duel victory = +30,000 region points**, the MVP **+6,000** — see the Region Duel manual.
- Day 6 (Defeat Enemies) falls into the Central City Clash, which also starts on Saturday. Whether a kill scores in both at once is **[assumption]** — but the fighting window is the same, so schedule troops and speedups for both.
