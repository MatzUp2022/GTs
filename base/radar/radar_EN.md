# Radar — Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- Radar missions grant big rewards but each has a **time limit** — finish them in time.
- **Refresh every 6 h**; the number of new missions grows with radar level (e.g. Lv. 8: 10 → Lv. 9: 11), as does the max active mission count (8 → 9).
- If the list is full at refresh, new missions go into **storage** — limited (e.g. **35**), so work stored missions off in time.
- **Auto-complete:** 10 stamina per mission.

## 2. Levels & Bonuses

- Level up by completing missions (e.g. **1,000** for Lv. 8 → 9); every level improves the radar's attributes.
- The **gold/iron/food bonus** grows with the level (Lv. 8: 10 % → Lv. 9: 15 %).
- **From radar Lv. 4:** [Help Allies] and [Supply Collection] can be completed instantly, including their reward series.
- Every level-up grants bonus missions (e.g. Lv. 8+: Diamond Mine ×1 · Gold Mine ×1 · Supply Collection ×2 · Clear Zombies ×1). **Without an alliance** at claim time you get no Help Allies missions.

## 4. SvS Strategy: Stockpile Until the Event

- The radar is **SvS-relevant** — place completions and rewards into the matching event phases (Arms Race / Region Duel).
- **Fill the storage before the event** (up to 35 stored missions): leave missions untouched and let the 6-hour refreshes fill the storage.
- **Complete them in bulk during the event:** with auto-complete (10 stamina per mission) stored missions convert quickly — save stamina beforehand.
- Watch the time limits: don't store so long that missions expire.

- [ ] Before SvS/Arms Race: storage filled, stamina saved
- [ ] During the event: stored missions completed in bulk
- [ ] No missions lost to the time limit
