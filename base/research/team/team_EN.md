# Research: Team — Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Structure of the Tree

- The team trees (Team 1–4) unlock in sequence — **Team 3 from Research Lab Lv. 20 + Team 2 ≥ 25 %** (see the Development manual). Team 1 carries the **"Combat Rec."** marker.
- Recurring building blocks, **5 levels** per tech:
  - **Team bonuses** (flag icon): DMG against zombies, zombie defense, then ATK (bullets) + DEF (shield) → HP (heart), plus gathering (pickaxe) and load bonuses (backpack).
  - **March speed** (boots): variants incl. toward zombies, toward stations and toward gathering, plus general march speed (arrow).
  - **Twin branches** with ATK/DEF/HP each: rifle icon (attack troop) and dome icon (garrison/defense).
  - **Milestones** (diamond frame, flame/lightning icon) cap the blocks.

## 2. Recommended Order

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Prioritize Team 1 as the combat tree (Combat Rec.) |
| 2 | The block's ATK/DEF/HP chain before the comfort techs |
| 3 | Pick march speed for your playstyle (zombie hunter vs. gatherer) |
| 4 | Push Team 2 to ≥ 25 % to unlock Team 3 (Lab 20) |

## 3. Links

- Completions count in the Arms Race **"Research Tech" phase at +1 point per tech CP**.
- Team bonuses apply wherever your teams fight: World Boss, Frenzied Boss, arenas, Region Duel.

## 4. Checklist

- [ ] Team 1 first, combat chain before comfort
- [ ] Team 2 ≥ 25 % for the Team 3 unlock
- [ ] March speed picked to match your role
- [ ] Completions placed into the Research Tech phase
