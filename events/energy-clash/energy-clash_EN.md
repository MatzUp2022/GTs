# Energy Clash — Event Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- An **Energy Airship crashes into the Central City of a Region**. Time and place are announced in advance (example from the preview: Region #10149 on 2026-09-26 at 04:10).
- **Energy Clash is not available while Region Duel is active** — the event and an SvS week never overlap.
- Several **Regions** compete; the Preview and Preparation phases name which ones are taking part.
- Phase sequence: **Preview → Contest round 1 → Preparation → Contest round 2 → Display** (settlement of the leaderboard rewards).
- **Energy Points** are the event currency; they unlock event chests (the visible track shows thresholds at **100K** and **150K**).

## 2. Two Rounds — and why winning one is enough

- The Contest Phase has **two rounds of 30 minutes each**.
- Each round's leaderboard counts **only the points earned inside that round**.
- Rewards, however, are issued on your **highest ranking across all rounds** — personal and alliance alike.
- → **You do not have to win both rounds, you have to win one.** Concentrating everything into a single round beats splitting the effort.
- Round 1 gone wrong is not a lost event: round 2 starts from zero and only the better result counts.
- The alliance ranking reward goes to **all alliance members**, not only to those who took part.

## 3. Point Sources

| Source | While gathering | On completion | At your base |
|--------|-----------------|---------------|--------------|
| Being inside the Crash Central Zone | **40 EP/s** | — | — |
| Blue Powercell | 40 EP/s | 300 EP | — |
| Purple Powercell | 80 EP/s | 1,500 EP + Purple Energy | **30 EP/s per unit** |
| Golden Powercell | 160 EP/s | 6,000 EP + Golden Energy | **120 EP/s per unit** |

- **Relocating into the zone is free:** moving from outside to any position inside **does not consume a relocation item**.
- Purple and Golden Energy are **carried back to your base and keep producing there**.
- **If your base is breached you lose Energy — but not the Energy Points already earned.** The bank is safe, the stream is not.

## 4. The Decisive Number: Finish Golden Early

Purple and Golden pay per second at your base for the rest of the round. The worth of one powercell therefore depends on **when** you finish it:

| Golden finished at | Base production to the end of the round | + instant | Total |
|--------------------|------------------------------------------|-----------|-------|
| minute 5 | 25 min × 120/s = 180,000 | 6,000 | **186,000** |
| minute 15 | 15 min × 120/s = 108,000 | 6,000 | **114,000** |
| minute 25 | 5 min × 120/s = 36,000 | 6,000 | **42,000** |

- The same Golden Powercell is worth **more than four times as much** early as it is late. Purple behaves identically at a quarter of the rate.
- Blue pays **300 once and nothing afterwards** — it is filler, not a target.
- → **Order: Golden first, Purple second, Blue only when nothing better is free.**
- Simply standing in the zone for a full round is **1,800 s × 40 = 72,000 EP**. **[assumption — the text says "Relocating into the Crash Central Zone grants 40 Energy Points per second" without stating explicitly that the income keeps running]**

## 5. Recommended Sequence

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Preview phase: note time and place, **relocate into the Crash Central Zone early** — it costs no relocation item |
| 2 | Round start: go for **Golden** powercells first, Purple second |
| 3 | Finish them **early** — every second the Energy sits at your base it produces |
| 4 | **Defend the base.** A breach costs Energy and with it the production stream |
| 5 | Preparation phase: the next location is announced — reposition in time |
| 6 | Round 2 as a fresh attempt: only the better of the two rounds counts |

## 6. Links to Other Events

- **Region Duel (SvS):** mutually exclusive — in an SvS week there is no Energy Clash. That also means the weekly plan never has to serve both at once.
- **Central City:** the airship lands on the Central City of a Region — who governs it is decided by the [Central City Battle](https://github.com/MatzUp2022/GTs/blob/main/events/central-city-battle/central-city-battle_EN.md).
- **Relocation items:** the free move applies to going **in**. Nothing is said about leaving again — budget an Alliance Relocation (500 diamonds, see the stores manual) for the way back. **[assumption]**
