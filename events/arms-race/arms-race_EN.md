# Arms Race — Event Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- Runs daily: **a new theme every 4 hours** (0:00, 4:00, 8:00, 12:00, 16:00, 20:00 server time) — 6 phases per day, **7-day rotation**.
- **Competition stage by base level** (e.g. Lv. 25–26); your opponents are a matched cross-server group ("Today's Opponent", 15 players).
- Points are earned **only for actions matching the current theme**.

## 2. Points & Chests

- Point chests at **6,250 / 12,500 / 30,000** points per phase.
- **Ranking rewards** within the opponent group: rank 1 / 2 / 3 / 4–5 / 6–15 (resource chests, plus puzzle/gift boxes for the top spots).
- Example **Research Tech**: +1 per tech CP · +10 per 1-min tech speedup · +30 per diamond in a purchased pack — tasks change per theme.
- Chest track in the event header (2/8/18): unlock condition not visible in the event info.

## 4. Strategy & SvS Link

- **Spend nothing on the side:** save speedups, construction, recruitment and research **for the matching phase**, then fire them all at once.
- Schedule big projects (expensive research, long construction) into the phase where they score.
- **Alliance Duel overlap:** on Tue–Fri the phases at 8:00 and 20:00 match the Alliance Duel theme of the day — one spend scores in both. See the [weekly plan](https://github.com/MatzUp2022/GTs/blob/main/strategy/weekly-plan/weekly-plan_EN.md).
- **SvS-relevant:** Arms Race actions also count in SvS — time big spends so they serve Arms Race **and** SvS at once.

- [ ] At least the first chest (6,250 points) collected
