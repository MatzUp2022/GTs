# Restricted Zone — Event Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- **Base Level 8+** required. Reached via the **Restricted Zone** tab (next to Arms Race and Apocalypse Fortress).
- **Only one difficulty can be selected per event** — the choice is binding for the whole run.
- A difficulty is unlocked only once you have **completed all challenges of the one below**.
- Each difficulty runs over **30 stages**; the opponent is the **Instructor** (e.g. difficulty 4, stage 30: "Nightfall Servant", Lv. 30, **recommended CP 8,531,000**).
- Two tracks in parallel: **Personal Challenge** and **Alliance Challenge**.

## 2. The Ladder — One Rung per Event

> "Once you complete all challenges of the current difficulty, higher difficulties will be available in the **next** Restricted Zone event."

- You therefore climb **at most one difficulty per event**, no matter how strong you are.
- → **Pick the highest difficulty you can clear all 30 stages of.** Anything else wastes the whole event:
  - too low — you clear it but the ladder was already open, so you gain no rung;
  - too high — you do not finish, and the rung stays closed.
- The recommended CP per stage is the yardstick. Compare it before choosing, not after.
- Missed events cannot be caught up: the ladder only ever advances when an event runs.

## 3. Personal Challenge

- You can face the Instructor **alone or by rallying allies** — a rally is allowed even for the personal track.
- Beating the Instructor unlocks the next stage within your difficulty.
- **Rewards are paid after the event ends**, for every personal challenge won — so an unfinished difficulty still pays for the stages you did clear.
- Example, difficulty 4 at 30/30 enemies: **21 EXP chests · 10 puzzle pieces · 410 medals · 90 diamonds**.

## 4. Alliance Challenge & Nightfall Elites

The alliance track does not open by strength but by **how many members got through**:

| Difficulty | Members who must clear stage 30 | Reward for **every** member |
|------------|--------------------------------|------------------------------|
| 5 | **15** | (partially visible on screen) |
| 6 | **18** | 3 × 2.5K · 17.3M iron · 17.3M bread · 10.9M gold |
| 7 | **20** | 4 × 2.5K · 22M · 22M · 14.1M |
| 8 | **20** | 4 × 2.5K · 24.2M · 24.2M · 15.5M |
| 9 | **20** | 4 × 2.5K · 26.6M · 26.6M · 17M |

*The 2.5K item is shown only as a badge with a count; the exact type is not legible on the screen.*

- Once enough commanders have cleared the matching difficulty, **R4 and above can apply to challenge the Nightfall Elites**.
- The Nightfall Elites stay for **24 hours**, are **extremely powerful**, and can only be brought down **by rally**.
- Beating them pays **all players in the alliance** when the event ends.

→ **The alliance reward hangs on head count, not on your top players.** Getting 15 members through difficulty 5 is worth more to the alliance than one member reaching difficulty 9. From difficulty 6 to 9 the resources per member rise by roughly half (17.3M → 26.6M) — but the entry price rises from 18 to 20 members who each have to clear 30 stages.

## 5. Recommended Sequence

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Check the recommended CP of stage 30 of the difficulty you are eyeing — that is the bar |
| 2 | Select the **highest difficulty you can clear completely**, and select it once: the choice is binding |
| 3 | Work the 30 stages; rally where solo no longer carries |
| 4 | R4/R5: count how many members are through and announce the alliance threshold (15 / 18 / 20) |
| 5 | Threshold reached → apply for the Nightfall Elites, then rally within the 24 hours |
| 6 | Rewards arrive after the event ends — for every stage won, not just for full clears |

## 6. Links to Other Events

- **Rally events:** the Instructor and the Nightfall Elites are fought by rally, like the Alliance Boss — the same hero setups and monster bonuses apply (see the team guide).
- **Alliance Duel:** hero EXP and medals from the personal rewards feed day 4 (Hero Upgrade); time the claim accordingly if the event ends near it. **[assumption — whether the payout time can be steered is not stated]**
- *The event's schedule (how often, on which days) is not visible on these screens — only a running timer of 07h35m.*

## 7. Checklist

- [ ] Recommended CP compared before selecting a difficulty
- [ ] Highest **fully clearable** difficulty selected — the choice is binding for the event
- [ ] All 30 stages worked through (otherwise no rung on the ladder)
- [ ] Rallies used where solo runs stall
- [ ] R4/R5: member count tracked against the threshold (15 / 18 / 20)
- [ ] Nightfall Elites applied for and rallied within the 24 hours
