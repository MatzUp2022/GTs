# Frenzied Boss — Event Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- Server event: **three bosses** arrive via portals near Central City — one each at **0:00, 6:00 and 12:00 server time**.
- **Base Level 5+** required; no single commander can beat them — the whole server fights together.
- Each boss has its own weaknesses — attacking them deals significantly more damage.
- At **24:00 server time** all undefeated bosses **flee** — deal your damage before that.

## 2. Attempt Economy

- **Unlimited attacks**, but only the **3 attempts with the highest damage** count toward the cumulative total.
- → Test freely first, then run **3 max runs** with all buffs.
- Once a boss is **weakened** (enough damage taken): claim the rewards immediately; your DMG record for the ranking can still be improved afterwards.
- **All-Out Attack** (unlocks in phases): all teams attack simultaneously — use it for concentrated damage.

## 3. Recommended Sequence

| Boss | HP (Lv. 1) | +50 % DMG from |
|------|------------|----------------|
| Giant Zombie | 40G | **Guardian** faction |
| Undead Zombie | 30G | **Ranger** faction |
| Frenzied Zombie | 25G | **Berserker** faction |

- Rebuild your lineup **per boss** around its weak faction — don't run all three with one standard team.
- Place your 3 scoring runs where your faction strength is greatest; check the ranking threshold (rank 100) on the event screen.

## 4. Rewards & Server Goal

- **Participation:** everyone who deals damage receives rewards **via mail** — more damage = better rewards.
- The **top 100** commanders by total damage receive extra ranking rewards.
- **All 3 bosses defeated** → next event features stronger bosses and better rewards; **none defeated** → weaker bosses and the richer follow-up rewards are unavailable.
