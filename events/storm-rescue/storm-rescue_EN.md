# Storm Rescue — Event Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- A run has **5 stages**. After each stage: proceed or exit.
- **Warning:** if you exit midway, your next attempt **starts again from stage 1**.
- **Clearing 5 stages in a row or failing a stage** → stage settlement.

## 2. Attempt Economy

- **3 completed event stages are enough for all achievement rewards** — more is not required.
- Don't exit voluntarily: restarting from stage 1 only costs time.
- → Keep going until the settlement comes (5 cleared or first defeat).

## 3. Recommended Sequence

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Start the challenge and play the stages in one go — don't leave midway |
| 2 | Get as far as possible; a defeat ends the run with a settlement |
| 3 | Claim the achievement rewards for 1 / 2 / 3 stages |

## 4. Rewards

| Stages completed | Rewards (among others) |
|------------------|------------------------|
| 1 | 2 badges · 6 EXP chests · 6 coin chests · 20 gems |
| 2 | 3 · 12 · 12 · 30 gems |
| 3 | 5 · 3 · 3 · 50 gems |

- Total preview on the event screen includes: 10 badges · 18 EXP chests · 18 coin chests · **100 gems**.

*Link to SvS or similar: not stated in the event info.*

## 5. Checklist

- [ ] Challenge started and played without exiting
- [ ] At least 3 stages completed
- [ ] All three achievement rewards claimed
- [ ] Remaining time checked on the event screen
