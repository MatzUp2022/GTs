# Alliance Boss — Event Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Phases & Flow

| Phase | What happens |
|-------|--------------|
| Selection | R4/R5 pick difficulty + start time — **min. 20 members** required |
| Preparation | Everyone donates resources → contribution rewards + DMG bonuses; donations continue even after goals are met |
| Battle | **30 minutes**; started manually by R4/R5, otherwise auto-start 30 min before server reset; the altar disappears and summons Demons |
| Settlement | Rewards based on alliance total DMG and personal DMG |

## 2. Ground Rules

- Demons can **only be attacked by rallies** — no solo attacks.
- Rallies started by **R4 or higher** gain an **additional DMG bonus** → prefer starting/joining their rallies.
- **Enable Join Offline** so your troops fight even while you're away.
- **Altar donations:** 1 donation = **×500 alliance contribution**; altar bonus at Lv. 5 = **+25 % DMG** to the Alliance Boss (max level).
- Higher alliance total DMG unlocks **higher difficulties** (e.g. Lv. 6 with a better rewards preview).

## 3. MVP Mechanic

- The player with the **highest personal DMG** multiplies the alliance rewards by **×1–10** (based on personal DMG) — up to **Reward ×10**.
- → Strongest players focus on maximum damage; everyone else fills the rallies.
