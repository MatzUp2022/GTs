# Central City Battle — Event Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- **Once a week**, lasting **8 hours**. Reached via the **Central City Battle** tab (next to World Boss); the screen counts down to the unlock.
- **Entry requirement: your alliance must occupy a Lv. 6 city.** No Lv. 6 city, no contest — the whole City Clash ladder below is the qualification.
- **The Leader of the winning alliance becomes Governor** of the region.
- The Central City area is a **Polluted Zone**: while you are in it you **cannot activate a shield, receive Radar missions or complete Outpost Tavern missions**.
- Sub-screens: Rules · Governor Authority · Region Office · Central City Rewards · Honor Points · View Central City.

## 2. The Road There — City Clash (Lv. 1 → Lv. 6)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Declaring war | **R5/R4** declare war on a city, which unlocks the contest for it |
| Parallelism | **One city at a time per alliance** — the ladder is serial, not parallel |
| First city | An alliance holding no city may declare war on **any Lv. 1 city** |
| Further cities | An alliance holding a city may only declare war on cities **adjacent** to one it already holds |
| Losses | City Clash causes **direct soldier deaths**, not wounded — exact rates in the city building description |
| First capture | Participants get **First Occupation Rewards**, every alliance member gets a **City Reward** |
| Holding | While a city is held, **all alliance members receive buffs** |
| Race targets | Rewards only for targets completed **within the time limit**; they are delivered by mail |

→ Because only one war can run at a time and every step must be adjacent, the climb from Lv. 1 to Lv. 6 is **a plan of weeks, not of one evening**. Start early enough that the Lv. 6 city stands before the battle week.

## 3. The Battle — 8 Hours, but 4 Decide It

- **First alliance to accumulate 4 hours of occupation wins immediately.**
- If nobody reaches 4 hours: the alliance with the **longest total occupation time** when the 8 hours end wins.
- → What counts is **accumulated time, not who holds it at the end**. Taking the city early and banking hours beats a late grab. Losing it in between is not fatal — the clock keeps what it has counted.
- **Direct soldier deaths** mean hospital capacity and healing speed do nothing here. Plan losses as replacements, not as repairs.

## 4. Region Office — Governor and Offices

The Region Office screen shows who holds which office, each with a timer next to the name:

| Level | Office |
|-------|--------|
| above | **Conqueror** · Military Commander · Grand Chancellor |
| centre | **Governor** — the leader of the alliance that won the Central City |
| below | CoS · Marshal · Minister of Health · Minister of Construction · Minister of Science · Minister of Domestic Affairs |

- Offices can be held by members of **different alliances** — the six ministries are distributed across the region, not reserved for the winner.
- **What the timers next to each name mean (term, cooldown or appointment lock) is not stated on the screen — check in game. [assumption]**
- **Conqueror** sits above the Governor and may be a cross-region title; the screen does not explain the relationship. **[assumption]**

## 5. Governor Commendation — 35,000 Diamonds to Hand Out

The Governor distributes commendations, in four tiers with fixed counts:

| Commendation | Count | Diamonds each | Total diamonds |
|--------------|-------|---------------|----------------|
| Governor | 1 | 5,000 | 5,000 |
| General | 5 | 2,000 | 10,000 |
| Sergeant | 10 | 1,000 | 10,000 |
| Soldier | 20 | 500 | 10,000 |
| **Sum** | **36** | | **35,000** |

- Every commendation also carries tickets and resource chests (Governor 20 · 48 · 48, General 10 · 24 · 24, Sergeant 10 · 16 · 16, Soldier 10 · 8 · 8) — only the diamond figure is unambiguously legible on the screen.
- A **Commendation Records** button logs who received what.
- → 36 packages worth 35,000 diamonds are **the Governor's real power**. Plan the distribution before the battle, not after: it is the currency you pay allies and your own top performers in.

## 6. Links to Other Events

- **Polluted Zone vs. Radar:** no Radar missions while you are in the area. On Alliance Duel day 1 a radar mission is worth 25,000 duel points — so do not park troops in the zone on a Radar Training day.
- **Polluted Zone vs. Tavern:** no Outpost Tavern missions either — that also stalls the treasure-map shards.
- **Direct deaths vs. Alliance Duel:** losses have to be retrained. Retraining scores on day 5 (Full Mobilization) — retrain there and the loss turns into duel points.
- **Region Duel (SvS):** the SvS manual describes a **Central City Clash** from Saturday, with its own personal point table (+54 per Lv. 8 kill in the Central City / Giant Battery, +27 in the Polluted Zone). **Whether that is this event, the same event during an SvS week, or a separate one is not resolved by these screens. ## 7. Checklist

- [ ] City Clash ladder planned — one war at a time, each step adjacent
- [ ] Lv. 6 city standing **before** the battle week
- [ ] Battle window (8 h) announced to the alliance in advance
- [ ] Take the city early and bank occupation hours (4 h ends it)
- [ ] Replacement troops planned — the dead do not come back from hospital
- [ ] No troops parked in the Polluted Zone on a Radar Training day
