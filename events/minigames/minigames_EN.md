# Minigames — Leave None · Soldier Assault · Cannon War (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- Three minigame modes on **level maps** with consecutive stages:
  **Leave None** (screws icon) · **Soldier Assault** (×3/−2) · **Cannon War** (cannon).
- Completed levels carry a **green check**; the current level is marked red; **elite levels** carry a mask icon.
- New modes/levels are flagged with **"New"** or a red dot — check in regularly.

## 2. Gameplay (Cannon War example)

- **Swipe** starts and steers the cannon down the lane.
- **Gates modify your shots:** ×2 / ×3 / ×4 multiply, −2 reduces — pick the route with the best multipliers and avoid minus gates.
- A **target with a hit count** waits at the end (e.g. truck "20") — if your multiplied firepower suffices, the level is cleared.
- Soldier Assault works on the same multiplier principle with soldiers instead of cannon shots.

## 3. Recommendation

- Levels are **repeatable until you succeed** — no visible attempt cost; just restart (refresh button).
- Take the elite levels (mask) as soon as they are reachable.
- *Rewards per level: not documented in the screenshots — check in game.*

## 4. Checklist

- [ ] All three modes checked for new levels ("New"/red dot)
- [ ] Multiplier route picked, minus gates avoided
- [ ] Elite levels cleared
