=== APOCALYPSE FORTRESS - BATTLE PLAN ===

CORE RULE: KILLS DO NOT WIN.
Kills = Personal Points only. NO Battlefield Points.
We win on HOLD TIME x POINTS/SEC. Nothing else.

POINT VALUES (Alliance/sec):
- 4x Field Hospital = 120 total  <-- BIGGEST PRIZE + healing
- Oil Well = 50 each             <-- main income
- Central District = 80 (+40 pers) - opens later
- Signal Station = 10 + BUFF: +10% on ALL our buildings
- Institute = 10 + BUFF: port every 60s instead of 120s
- Arms Workshop = 10 + BUFF: +15% our heroes
- Barracks = 10 + BUFF: -15% enemy heroes

Hospitals are worth MORE than Central. Everyone forgets
them. That is our edge. Take all 4 and never lose them.

--- ROLES ---

STRONG (top 5-6) = SPEARHEAD
Break Oil Wells + Central. NEVER sit on a building.
Break -> mid player occupies -> you move on. Always
fighting, always porting.

MID (8-10) = HOLDERS / 2nd WAVE
Move in the second a building breaks and occupy it.
Hold Signal, Institute, Workshop, Barracks.
Join strong players' rallies.

WEAKER (5-7) = HOSPITAL CREW + COLLECTORS
NEVER fight strong enemies - you just feed their points.
Your jobs: hold all 4 Field Hospitals, gather Energy
Tanks, grab Supply Crates after every break, scout.

CAN'T LEAVE SAFE ZONE? (no troops / no port / enemy
too strong) -> stay in Safe Zone and keep feeding Oil
Wells + crystal gathering. Still full value to us.

--- PHASES ---

PHASE 1 (from second 0):
- 2 squads INSTANTLY to INSTITUTE + SIGNAL STATION.
  Both buffs compound over the whole battle. Early = huge.
- Strong players -> OIL WELLS.
- Weaker players -> ALL 4 FIELD HOSPITALS. Usually
  undefended = 120/s for free.

PHASE 2 (Central opens):
- WORKSHOP + BARRACKS FIRST, THEN CENTRAL.
  +15% us / -15% them = 30% swing. Do not run straight
  at Central without the buffs - that fight is the most
  expensive one on the map.
- Strong break Central, Mid holds it.

PHASE 3 (Energy Tanks):
- Strong stay on Central. Mid + Weak collect tanks.

LAST 3 MINUTES - OVERFLOW RAID:
Break enemy buildings they held a long time. Weak
players follow immediately with scouts and collect
the Supply Crates. Highest value window of the match.

--- PORT DISCIPLINE ---

Take Institute first = 60s port cycle.
PORT ONLY FOR 3 REASONS:
1. Starting an attack on a new target
2. Retreating when outnumbered (do NOT die)
3. Switching after your target is secured

Never march long distances. Never port just because
it is off cooldown - keep it ready for retreat.

--- HEALING ---

Field Hospital heals 15 troops / 10s = 90/min each.
FIRST BATTLE: check if multiple hospitals STACK.
If yes = 360/min and all 4 are mandatory targets.

Heal rate is our hard limit, not troop count.
Weaker players: PORT OUT of lost fights, do not
"fight to the end". Dead troops make 0 points AND
block the heal queue for our strong players.

--- COMMAND ---

One Battle Lead (R4/R5) calls only 3 things:
TARGET SWITCH / RALLY UP / RETREAT.
Everything else runs on your role. No debating in chat.
