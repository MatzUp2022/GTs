## 1. Overview

- Store bar in game: **Diamond Store · VIP Shop · Alliance Store · Honor Store** (…).
- The **Goods Refresh Ticket** (100 diamonds) refreshes the offering.

## 3. Value Assessment

- **Resources are cheap** (50 diamonds each) — useful as filler before Arms Race phases.
- **Relocations:** to move to your alliance the **Alliance Relocation (500)** suffices — three times cheaper than the freely placeable Premium Relocation (1,500). Important for the **Region Duel** (Saturday: relocating into the enemy region).
- Compare recruit tickets (400 each) with event offers before buying here.
