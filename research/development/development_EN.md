# Research: Development — Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Tech System (Overview)

- Research happens in the **Research Lab**; categories with their own progress include Development, Economy, Hero, Soldier, Team 1/2/4, Alliance Duel, Trade Truck, Super Soldier.
- The game marks recommendations: **"Combat Rec."** (e.g. Team 1) and **"Economy Rec."** (e.g. Trade Truck).
- **Unlocks:** Team 3 from **Lab Lv. 20** + Team 2 ≥ 25 %; Siege and Defense Specialization from **Lab Lv. 21** + Super Soldier ≥ 40 %.
- **Two research slots** with their own timers — keep both busy at all times.

## 2. Structure of the Development Tree

- Recurring blocks: **High-speed Construction** → Hospital + Barracks Expansion → **Research Speed Up** → Rapid Healing + Training Speed Up → **milestone** (e.g. Professional Craftsman) — then tier 2 with the same techs at higher costs.
- Every tech has **5 levels**.

## 4. Priorities & Arms Race Link

- **Max Research Speed Up first** (+10 % research speed) — it accelerates everything after, then High-speed Construction.
- Raise Hospital/Rapid Healing before SvS weekends (healing in the Central City Clash).
- **Arms Race:** the "Research Tech" phase grants **+1 point per tech CP** — the CP column above is literally your Arms Race score. Finish big researches there on purpose.
- Use the recommendation markers (Combat/Economy Rec.) for the other categories.
