# Research: Hero — Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Structure of the Tree

- Faction blocks **in sequence**: Guardian (tower emblem) → Ranger (wings) → Berserker (claws) → heroes in general (cap emblem).
- Per block: **milestone** (flame icon, diamond frame) → **Weapon Enhancement (hero ATK)** + **Armor Enhancement (hero DEF)** → **Physique Enhancement (hero HP)** — each tech has **5 levels**.
- A block must be researched through before the next one opens.

## 2. Values (example from a later block)

| Tech | Effect Lv. 1 → 5 | CP Lv. 1 → 5 |
|------|------------------|--------------|
| Weapon Enhancement (hero ATK) | +2 → 10 % | 14,842 → 140,516 |
| Armor Enhancement (hero DEF) | +2 → 10 % | 14,842 → 140,516 |
| Physique Enhancement (hero HP) | +2 → 10 % | 16,558 → 146,160 |

- Durations without boosts: roughly **9–12 days per level** (complete-now costs 25,086–56,186 diamonds).

## 3. Arms Race / SvS / Trials Link

- **CP bombs:** a single completion worth e.g. 140,516 CP scores **140,516 points** in the Arms Race "Research Tech" phase — all three point chests from one research. Time the completion into that phase (save speedups).
- The faction bonuses feed directly into the **Faction Trial** and **Frenzied Boss** (Guardian/Ranger/Berserker) and lift your teams for World Boss & arenas.

## 4. Checklist

- [ ] Current faction block researched through (order Guardian → Ranger → Berserker)
- [ ] Long hero researches in the second slot, short techs in the first
- [ ] Big hero tech completions placed into the Research Tech phase
- [ ] Speedups reserved for completion day
