# Research: Alliance Duel — Guide (Last Light)

## 1. Structure of the Tree

- The tree consists of **incentive techs with 10 levels each** that raise your **point yield in the Alliance Duel**, plus **Super Reward milestones** (chest icon, 1 level, 5,048 CP).
- Four incentives are documented — all on the identical scale:

| Incentive | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Enemy Kill Incentive | more points per enemy killed |
| Training Incentive | more points per soldier trained |
| Construction Incentive | more points per building CP |
| Research Incentive | more points per tech CP |

- Example research times: roughly **2.5–3 days per level** (complete now 7,198–8,898 diamonds).

## 3. Priorities & Links

- **Research Incentive first:** +50 % on tech CP points multiplies your big research completions (the hero "CP bombs") in the Alliance Duel.
- Then by playstyle: **Training** (constant trainers) or **Enemy Kill** (SvS fighters); Construction rides along.
- Alliance Duel victories grant **+30,000 region points** in the Region Duel (SvS), the MVP +6,000 — this tree pays directly into the SvS week.
- The completions themselves also count in the Arms Race "Research Tech" phase (+1 point per CP).

## 4. Checklist

- [ ] Research Incentive leveled before the other incentives
- [ ] The incentive matching your point source (training/kills) next
- [ ] Super Reward milestones collected
- [ ] Completions placed into the Research Tech phase
