# Adventure — stage campaign & AFK income (Last Light)

## 1. Ground Rules

- A continuous **stage map**; **Challenge** starts the fight for the current stage with your five deployed heroes.
- Before the fight both **CP values** appear at the top (example stage 308: your 8.74M against 4.49M) — the best indicator of whether a stage is doable.
- In the line-up the icons filter by **ALL / Guardian / Ranger / Berserker** — the quickest way to assemble a **mono-faction team for +20 % HP/ATK/DEF** (see the team guide).
- **Star chest** (0/15): collect stars from cleared stages and empty the chest.

## 2. AFK Income — the real value

- Accrues automatically, offline too. Example rates at stage 308:

| Resource | Rate |
|----------|------|
| Iron | 165.6K/h |
| Bread | 165.6K/h |
| Gold | 111.6K/h |

- **Storage only holds 14.57 hours.** Anything beyond that is lost.
- → **Claim at least twice a day**, ideally morning and evening. Claiming once a day throws away roughly 40 % of the daily yield.
- *Whether the rates rise with stage progress was not stated in the screenshots — compare in game after a jump in stages.*

## 3. Recommended Sequence

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Claim the AFK rewards before the 14.57 h fill up |
| 2 | Check the line-up — five heroes of one faction where possible |
| 3 | Start the challenge; on a loss don't just repeat, raise hero levels, stars or equipment first |
| 4 | Empty the star chest at 15 stars |

## 4. Links

- **Leaderboard:** "Cleared Stages Count" is a board of its own (see the profile manual).
- The AFK resources are permanent income for research and construction — and therefore indirectly points in the matching **Arms Race** phases.
