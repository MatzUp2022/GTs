# Hero Rankings — who hits hardest, who holds longest (Last Light)

## 1. How to Read These Lists

- **Damage = skill multiplier × the hero's ATK.** What is ranked here are the documented multipliers, not finished damage — a 670 % skill on a weak hero loses to a 512 % skill on a strong one.
- **Star effects are not added in.** The game shows them as a ladder (+30/70/120/185/270 %) without saying whether a step replaces the one before it or stacks on top. Ranking by base values keeps the order honest; the ladder per hero is in the hero database.
- **The Hero tab shows ATK · HP · DEF · Soldier Capacity even for heroes you have not obtained.** That is where a true stat ranking comes from. So far exactly one hero is documented that way: Rosa at Lv. 150 / 5★ — ATK 44.0K · HP 388.0K · DEF 5.2K · 433 soldiers · CP 788,715.
- **For mitigation, read the scope, not the number.** All damage / physical only / energy only / monsters only are four different things, and monster-only is worth nothing in the arena.

## 7. What That Means for Your Build Order

- **The biggest single number belongs to a Support.** Clara's Vital Drain hits for 670 %, but it picks a random target. The biggest **aimable** hit is Rosa's Killshot Lock at 614 %, and it is the only one that splashes onto the back row.
- **Against a single boss, random-target skills become single-target skills.** That flips the ranking: in World Boss, Rosa's 1,188 % per cast (1,980 % once ★4 raises the attack limit to 5) and Ella's 936 % all land on the same body — far ahead of any single hit.
- **AoE only pays where there are several enemies:** Jessica and Admira for Frenzied Boss, Storm Rescue and the arenas; against one boss their all-enemy skills are just a small single hit.
- **Crit multiplies everything above it.** Rosa (+30 %) and Jessica (+26 %) are the crit core; stars on them are worth more than stars on a hero with a flat ATK passive.
- **Two lists, two purposes.** The mitigation and monster-damage entries are the World Boss / rally / radar kit, the single-hit and crit entries are the arena kit — the same split as S vs. S+ in the hero database.

## 8. Checklist

- [ ] Own ATK values read off the Hero tab before trusting a multiplier ranking
- [ ] Boss fight → multi-hit hero fielded (Rosa, Ella, Sagitta), not a single-hit nuker
- [ ] Several enemies → AoE fielded (Jessica, Admira, Eileen)
- [ ] Arena defense → only Admira, Viper, Marcus, Valkyra count as mitigation
- [ ] Monster events → the monster-bonus heroes fielded, not the S+ arena kits
- [ ] Stars spent on the crit carries first, then on the flat ATK passives
